Safety & Seamless Transition:
Aligned with SFUSD After-School Standards

We ensure a smooth handoff from regular classes to our after-school program. If students need to attend other on-campus activities, our teachers will safely escort them to designated venues on site. We take full responsibility for student supervision and transportation within the school campus to keep every child safe.

  • Live Scan fingerprint background check (DOJ-compliant)
  • Tuberculosis (TB) risk assessment and health clearance
  • Mandated Reporter training (child abuse prevention, per California state law)
  • CPR and first aid certification
  • Child safety and youth protection training
  • Emergency response and safety procedure training
  • Student arrival and dismissal protocols
  • Classroom supervision and staff conduct guidelines
We believe child safety, professional accountability, and family trust are the foundation of any successful after-school program.

Experienced Local Educators

Our teaching team consists of experienced local educators with deep knowledge of Bay Area school culture and after-school programs. They bring hands-on experience from SFUSD partner schools and local community schools across San Francisco.

Our team understands SFUSD’s academic standards, cultural norms, and school-family partnership practices. We design our Mandarin curriculum to support diverse learners while building cultural competence and long-term confidence in the language.

High Mandarin Proficiency Standards

All lead teaching staff demonstrate advanced Mandarin language proficiency, verified by the Putonghua Shuiping Ceshi (PSC) Level 2-A certification (the national standard for professional Mandarin instruction).

For educators with strong local teaching experience who are in the process of completing full certification, we conduct rigorous internal assessments of pronunciation and classroom language use before they begin working with students. We believe consistent, clear, and natural Mandarin modeling is essential for building students’ long-term confidence in the language.

Community-Centered Program & Family Trust

We select staff based not only on professional credentials and safety clearances but also on their ability to connect with students, communicate effectively, and build trusting relationships with families.

As a community-based program, we actively incorporate feedback from local families and school communities into our program design and staff selection process.

More Than an After-School Program

We are building a safe, professional, and inclusive Mandarin enrichment program designed to support children academically, socially, and culturally. Our goal is to help students grow in language and cultural competence while ensuring families feel informed, connected, and supported every step of the way.
