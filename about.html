Unity Mandarin Enrichment Center

Built by Families, Rooted in Community

Unity Mandarin Enrichment Center (同心中文) is a community-based nonprofit organization founded by experienced Mandarin educators, youth program leaders, and SFUSD parent volunteers. We are dedicated to partnering with local elementary schools to support students’ learning, well-being, and meaningful engagement beyond the school day.

🏮 Learn Mandarin,
Connect Cultures.

🌱 Our Mission

We aim to provide a warm, inclusive, and student-centered after-school environment where children can develop Mandarin language skills while building confidence, curiosity, and positive peer relationships.

Our programs are designed to complement the school day by offering structured, engaging, and developmentally appropriate learning experiences for K–5 students.

📚 Program Focus

Our Mandarin enrichment program supports students through:

  • Age-appropriate Mandarin language development with a focus on clear and natural pronunciation.
  • Interactive learning experiences that encourage participation, communication, and engagement.
  • Consistent routines that help students feel safe, comfortable, and supported.
  • Opportunities for collaborative learning and positive peer interaction.
  • Activities that connect language learning with culture, creativity, and real-life contexts.

🤝 School & Family Partnership

We value strong and ongoing collaboration with school staff and families. Our team prioritizes:

  • Clear and consistent communication with school leadership and program coordinators.
  • Responsive partnership with families and caregivers.
  • Alignment with school expectations, schedules, and campus culture.
  • A supportive and respectful approach to student needs and school community values.

🧡 Our Commitment to Students

We believe children learn best when they feel safe, supported, and encouraged. Our instructors are committed to creating a positive classroom environment where students feel comfortable participating, expressing themselves, and developing confidence in Mandarin.

🌍 Community-Based Approach

As a nonprofit rooted in San Francisco’s westside school communities, we are committed to being a reliable and collaborative partner that supports both students and the broader school community.

We strive to contribute positively to the schools we serve through consistency, care, and long-term partnership.

Yanhong (Amy) Wang (王老师)

Founder & Program Director

Amy is a seasoned Mandarin educator and an SFUSD parent with over 15 years of classroom teaching experience in Mainland China, supplemented by more than four years of on-site expanded learning leadership within the San Francisco Unified School District. She has previously served as both Site Coordinator and Lead Mandarin Instructor for large-scale youth enrichment programs.

As a mother successfully raising bilingual children in San Francisco, Amy has firsthand expertise navigating the district's specialized pathways. Her eldest son achieved a perfect score (100) on the official SFUSD Mandarin Pathway Assessment during his 6th-grade transition, securing placement in the competitive Mandarin Dual Language Immersion (DLI) Program at Aptos Middle School. Most recently, he gained admission to the prestigious Lowell High School through the highly selective Band 1 merit-based admissions.

This personal journey fuels Amy’s deep understanding of the unique challenges immigrant and overseas families face in preserving heritage language proficiency and navigating academic milestones. She firmly believes that high-quality bilingual education requires a holistic blend of rigorous instruction, emotional support, and culturally responsive care.
