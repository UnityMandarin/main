Who We Are

Unity Mandarin Enrichment Center (同心中文)

Unity Mandarin Enrichment Center (同心中文) is a community-based nonprofit organization founded by experienced Mandarin educators, youth program leaders, and active SFUSD parents. Rooted in San Francisco’s westside school communities, we are committed to creating a warm, inclusive, and supportive environment where children can grow through language, culture, creativity, and meaningful connection.

Our after-school and enrichment programs are designed to support students’ Mandarin language development while also encouraging confidence, curiosity, collaboration, and social-emotional growth. We value strong partnerships with families and schools and strive to provide a positive space where children feel engaged, supported, and connected to their community.

Learn Mandarin, Connect Cultures.

Built by Families, Rooted in Community

We are dedicated to creating a warm, welcoming, and student-centered environment where Mandarin learning is supported alongside student well-being, positive family partnerships, and meaningful community connection.

Supporting Students, Families, & Community

We believe children thrive when they feel safe, supported, and genuinely cared for. As a community-based nonprofit, we are committed to supporting students, partnering with families, and contributing positively to the school communities we serve.

Educational Philosophy

We believe true language acquisition happens naturally through trust, consistency, joyful engagement, and authentic daily immersion. Our pedagogy moves beyond rote memorization and repetitive worksheets, emphasizing:

  • ✓ A rigorous foundation in Mandarin with a focus on clear, authentic pronunciation.
  • ✓ A positive, respectful classroom culture anchored by predictable routines.
  • ✓ The development of student confidence, cultural pride, and peer-to-peer connection.
  • ✓ Long-term, transparent collaborative relationships with families.

Meet Our Team

王

Yanhong (Amy) Wang (王老师)

Founder & Program Director

Amy is a seasoned Mandarin educator and an SFUSD parent with over 15 years of classroom teaching experience in Mainland China, supplemented by more than four years of on-site expanded learning leadership within the San Francisco Unified School District. She has previously served as both Site Coordinator and Lead Mandarin Instructor for large-scale youth enrichment programs.

As a mother successfully raising bilingual children in San Francisco, Amy has firsthand expertise navigating the district's specialized pathways. Her eldest son achieved a perfect score (100) on the official SFUSD Mandarin Pathway Assessment during his 6th-grade transition, securing placement in the competitive Mandarin Dual Language Immersion (DLI) Program at Aptos Middle School. Most recently, he gained admission to the prestigious Lowell High School through the highly selective Band 1 merit-based admissions.

This personal journey fuels Amy’s deep understanding of the unique challenges immigrant and overseas families face in preserving heritage language proficiency and navigating academic milestones. She firmly believes that high-quality bilingual education requires a holistic blend of rigorous instruction, emotional support, and culturally responsive care.

陈

Ms. Chen (陈老师)

Founder & Site Coordinator

Ms. Chen is a dedicated Mandarin educator and an experienced SFUSD parent with years of classroom experience. A passionate advocate for family-centered learning and bilingual literacy, Ms. Chen successfully guided her own child through the local public school pipeline to enrollment at the University of California, Berkeley.

Known for her warmth, patience, and highly approachable teaching style, she is deeply cherished by families. Ms. Chen operates on the conviction that impactful education is built on a foundation of mutual trust and emotional security.

高

Founder & Instructor

Ms. Gao brings over two years of hands-on Mandarin instructional experience within SFUSD after-school spaces. Widely recognized for her high-energy classroom presence and infectious positivity, she specializes in interactive, student-centered teaching methodologies.
