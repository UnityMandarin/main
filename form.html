📌 项目说明

📌 Program Highlights

• 上课频率与时间：本项目为每周 5 天的系统沉浸式中文班（暂不设 2 天或 3 天的选修），招收 K 班及以上学龄儿童。我们会根据不同学校的具体放学时间来对接课后班的开始时间，家长放学后直接在校园内接孩子即可。 Frequency and Time: This program is a 5-day-a-week, full-immersion systematic Mandarin class. To ensure the best language acquisition effect and continuity of learning, this program currently only provides the 5-day full-time choice (part-time or partial-day electives are temporarily not open), enrolling children in Kindergarten and above. We will seamlessly connect with the specific dismissal time of each public elementary school, and parents can simply pick up directly from the school campus after class.

• 关于拼音与教材选择（科学分级教学）： About Pinyin and Textbook Selection (Scientific Level-Based Teaching):
- Level 1（幼儿启蒙阶段）： 考虑到这个年龄段的孩子正在公立学校学习英语拼读（Phonics），为了完全避免英文字母和拼音发音搞混，我们在这个阶段不教拼音。教材选择《幼儿汉语》，更符合幼儿的认知特点。通过儿歌、互动游戏、创意手工和角色扮演，让孩子把中文“玩”进大脑，细心呵护与启发孩子对中文的兴趣，培养自然流利的听说语感。
- Levels 2 - 6（系统进阶阶段）： 当孩子具备一定的听说基础后，我们将无缝衔接海外使用最广、最成熟的暨南大学《中文》教材。开始系统拼音识字、阅读理解和汉字书写输出，稳步全面提升听说读写能力。 - Level 1 (Early Childhood Enlightenment Stage): Considering that children in this age group are naturally absorbing phonics in public schools, to completely avoid confusion between English letters and Pinyin pronunciation, our Level 1 stage does not learn Pinyin. The textbook selects You'er Hanyu, which better fits the cognitive characteristics of young children. Through singing, interactive games, creative crafts, and role-play, children "play" Mandarin into their brains, carefully nurturing and inspiring their interest in Mandarin, and developing a natural, fluent sense of listening and speaking.
- Levels 2 - 6 (Systemic Progression Stage): When children have a certain foundation in listening and speaking, we will seamlessly connect with the Jinan University Zhongwen textbooks, which are the most widely used and mature overseas. We will begin systematic Pinyin literacy, reading comprehension, and Mandarin writing output, steadily and comprehensively improving listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills.

• 关于师资与发音标准：我们的 Lead Instructors（首席讲师）与骨干教师团队均持有中国国家普通话二级甲等证书（Level 2-A，国内专业语文老师与播音标准）。我们的教学团队成员也通过了系统的发音考核，保持高标准的教学输出，让孩子从小接触纯正流利的中文表达。 About Faculty and Pronunciation Standards: Our Lead Instructors and core faculty members all hold Official Advanced Mandarin Certifications (Level 2-A, meeting the pronunciation and teaching standards of professional elementary school Chinese teachers). Our teaching team members have also passed systematic pronunciation assessments and maintain high standards of teaching output, allowing children to connect with standard, fluent Mandarin expressions from an early age.
