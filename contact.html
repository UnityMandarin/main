Frequently Asked Questions

What are your operating hours?

We are open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We follow the standard SFUSD holiday calendar.

Do you offer part-time enrollment?

Yes, we offer custom tracks depending on availability. Please fill out the contact form indicating your preferred days, and our admissions team will assist you.

How do I schedule a tour?

Tours are currently by appointment only. You can shoot us an email at info@unitymandarin.org or use the contact form to request a time.

Where are you located?

We are proudly located in the heart of San Francisco, CA. Full address and parking instructions are provided upon scheduling a visit.
