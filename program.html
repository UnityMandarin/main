⚠️ Important Guidelines for UMC After-School Parents

🎯 Day 1 Milestone: Free Mandarin Assessment & Track Assignment

To ensure differentiated instructions, our teaching faculty hosts diagnostic Chinese literacy and conversational assessments for every newly enrolled child on the first day of the academic fall semester. This assessment maps out current proficiency metrics to balance class structures, guaranteeing that your child moves into a precisely calibrated learning environment from Day 2 onwards.

1. Eligible Grade Groups & Restrictions

This campus-integrated program provides specialized services for children from Kindergarten through 5th Grade. Due to facilities parameters and licensing supervisor-to-student ratios, we are currently unable to accept TK (Transitional Kindergarten) students.

2. Core Linguistic Continuity Requirement

To secure a complete immersive ecosystem and prevent pacing disruptions, the UMC program does not offer fragmented 2-day or 3-day enrollment fractions. Consistent 5-day high-frequency immersion is critical for long-term native processing fluency.
