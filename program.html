📚 Official Curricula & Academic Standards

Level 1 (Pre-K / Kindergarten)

Pre-Reading via 《幼儿汉语》

To avoid confusing your child while they absorb English Phonics at school, we strictly skip Pinyin in Level 1. We learn through singing, role-play, and hands-on crafts to build natural speaking fluency.

Level 2 - 6 (Systematic Advancement)

Full Immersion via Jinan University 《中文》

Comprehensive training in Pinyin, character recognition, reading comprehension, and essay writing once English Phonics foundations are secure.
