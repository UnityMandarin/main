⚠️ Key Logistical Guidelines & Program Parameters

🎯 Day 1 Benchmark: Complimentary Placement Diagnostic

To uphold academic precision, every incoming student undergoes comprehensive language mapping assessments on the first operational day. Standard adjusted leveled classroom trajectories deploy actively starting from Day 2 based on finalized metric outputs.

1. Restricted Grades & Compliance Ratios

Instruction configurations focus explicitly on grades K-5. Due to institutional facility limits and strict regulatory supervisory ratios, applications for TK (Transitional Kindergarten) cannot be accommodated under current standard licensing parameters.

2. Core Linguistic Continuity Requirement

To ensure deep processing continuity, part-time fractions (such as 2-day or 3-day variations) are omitted. Consistent 5-day interaction is mandatory to secure the proper daily immersion density required for programmatic fluency milestones.

3. Rate Structuring and Financial Finality

Calculations displayed dynamically describe baseline core recurring parameters. Crisis drop-in buffers are set to $25/hour. Formal, binding contracts and promotional terms are established exclusively through individual operational admissions files provided before seasonal launches.
