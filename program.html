⚠️ Key Program Guidelines

🎯 Day 1: Free Professional Placement Assessment

Every new student receives a complimentary Mandarin diagnostic assessment on their first day. This ensures accurate placement into the right leveled instruction group, so your child is learning at exactly the right pace starting Day 2.

1. Grades K–5 Only (TK Not Available)

Our program serves students in grades Kindergarten through 5th grade. Due to facility capacity and licensing requirements, we are currently unable to accommodate Transitional Kindergarten (TK) students.

2. Full 5-Day Enrollment Required

To maintain the consistent daily immersion that makes our Mandarin program effective, we require full 5-day-per-week enrollment. Part-time schedules (2-day or 3-day options) are not available at this time.

3. Tuition & Drop-In Rates

Monthly tuition shown reflects the standard recurring program cost. Occasional drop-in care is available at $25/hour. Final pricing and any promotional terms are confirmed through your official enrollment agreement before the season begins.
