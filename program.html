🤝 3. 双职工支持与晚接宽容政策 / Dual-Income Support & Late Pickup Grace Policy

为了照顾父母双工作的家庭，以及我们对家长的体谅与贴心，在遇到特殊情况偶尔晚接娃时，UMC 绝不会实施严苛的迟到罚款制度，期间产生的一切看护运营费用均由我们学园全额承担。关于个人条款和各季节的具体作息安排，将通过您家庭的 Brightwheel 档案进行清晰、规范的管理。
We deeply understand and respect the intense rhythms of SF's dual-income households. Balancing demanding careers with family life is a beautiful but unpredictable journey, and we are here to support you every step of the way. In the spirit of true flexibility and community care, UMC does not enforce rigid, punitive fine structures for those rare, unexpected moments when you are running late. Should an occasional delay arise, any operational buffer costs incurred will be fully absorbed by our academy. Complete alignment on individual terms and seasonal scheduling parameters will remain neatly managed within your family's Brightwheel admissions file.

※ 计费及日常说明：UMC 严格采用诚实自然月(Calendar Month)计费，月费已全额包含 Brightwheel 平台的技术授权费。有关作业看护，本校讲师负责多学科家庭作业的日常答疑与辅导，不作为"100%全部完成或绝对无误"之刚性承诺。本校可通过 Brightwheel 系统配合家长导出合规的 Dependent Care FSA 报销凭证。
* Monthly billing follows a standard calendar-month cycle, fully incorporating all Brightwheel platform licensing fees. Academic coaching provides multi-subject homework guidance and Q&A; it does not constitute a rigid guarantee of 100% completion or perfect accuracy. Tax-ready statements for Dependent Care FSA/Child Care Tax Credits can be conveniently exported directly via Brightwheel.
